MTG Graph

An interactive map of 33,683 Magic: The Gathering cards placed by semantic similarity. Cards close together share mechanics, themes, or text patterns. Embeddings via OpenAI text-embedding-3-small, projected with UMAP, clustered with HDBSCAN, labels via GPT-4o-mini.

2D viewer →

Fast and dense. Best for searching, filtering, and watching the timeline animate from 1993 → today.

3D viewer →

Orbit through the embedding space. Same filters, same data, different perspective on cluster structure.

Initial load fetches a ~95 MB JSON payload (gzipped to ~15-20 MB over the wire). Cached after first visit. Source: github.com/Coach-House/mtg-graph
